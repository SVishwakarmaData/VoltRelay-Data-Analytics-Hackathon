# VoltRelay — Battery-Swapping Network Analytics

## Project Overview

A data analytics project completed as part of the **Gradient Learnings Data Analytics Hackathon (November 2026)**.

Analyzed approximately **3.87 million battery-swapping events** across six Indian cities to investigate operational performance, identify failure patterns, and develop data-driven business recommendations.

## Business Problem

Battery-swapping network reliability can be affected by battery availability, charging performance, station-level failures, and customer waiting times.

The objective was to investigate these operational challenges and identify opportunities to improve network performance.

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- SQL
- DuckDB
- Google Colab
- Power BI

## Key Findings

- Overall non-completion rate was approximately 5.96%.
- Delhi NCR recorded a 7.35% non-completion rate.
- Jaipur recorded the highest failure rate among the six cities.
- Gen1 chargers had a higher non-completion rate than Gen2 and Gen3 chargers.
- Several underperforming stations contributed disproportionately to unsuccessful swaps.
- May 2025 recorded a 9.91% non-completion rate.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize maintenance at stations with high failure rates.
- Investigate and improve underperforming Gen1 chargers.
- Improve battery availability and charging operations.
- Monitor waiting times and station-level performance.
- Establish regular operational KPI monitoring.
